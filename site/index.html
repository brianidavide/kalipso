Before your first call.

Will it appear when I share my screen?+

It can. Screen capture behavior depends on your desktop and sharing method. We do not promise universal invisibility. Check the view from another participant before relying on your setup.

Is speech recognition included?+

No. Native speech libraries and models are installed separately, with explicit setup commands. You can read and edit manually without them. Choosing an engine in Settings does not download it.

Is this ready for everyday use?+

This is a development preview, with support for KDE Plasma and GNOME. Recognition can lose your place, and desktop scripts currently last for the session. Keep a copy of your script and test on your own hardware. See the installation guide.

Where are the AI answers?+

They are outside this first release. The current work is a teleprompter: editing, reading and local voice following.
