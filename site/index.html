FROM YOUR NOTES TO YOUR NEXT WORDS

Write it. Open it.

Use Kalipso when you want to follow a prepared script without looking down at a separate document.

  1. 01

OPTIONAL VOICE FOLLOWING

Speak naturally.
Keep audio local.

Kalipso uses speech recognition to match what you say to your script. It does not read the script aloud. No account or audio upload is required for voice following.

Speech assets may need an initial download. After setup, recognition runs on your computer with a supported engine and language.

Speech setup for Linux ↗

Moonshine & Vosk

Local models

Choose an engine and language model. The Linux installer downloads verified runtimes and models for x86_64: Moonshine in English, German, Spanish, Japanese and Chinese; Vosk in those and Italian. Other systems need compatible libraries and models supplied separately.

System speech

Mac & Windows

The native previews can use supported on-device speech on macOS or an installed Windows desktop recognizer. Available languages depend on the operating system.

Native speech requirements ↗

Choosing an engine does not download it. You can always start with manual reading.

Prefer a standalone app on Linux?

The standalone app has the full feature set: script library, speaker-note import, a movable and mirrorable reader, and a phone remote. Build it or make a .deb package from the source.

Before you use it.

Does it work with my call or recording app?+

Kalipso is a separate reading window; it does not need to be embedded in a particular meeting app. Window placement and capture behavior vary by desktop. Test your actual call or recording setup before relying on it.

Will others see it when I share my screen?+

On Windows the standalone app asks the system to leave the reader out of screen shares and recordings, and KDE Plasma 6.6 or later can hide it too. macOS offers no reliable way for an app to do this, so share a single window there. In every case, check the view from another participant, or the recorded video, before your presentation.

Can it read PowerPoint or Impress notes?+

Yes. The standalone app imports the speaker notes of .pptx and .odp files, one section per slide, so you can jump between slides while reading. Following the slide shown in a running slideshow is not available yet.

Can the reader adapt its colors?+

Yes: choose light, dark or an automatic mode. The color source depends on the platform. On KDE/Wayland, you explicitly select a window to share with Kalipso; it samples that window overall. Images stay in memory and are not saved or uploaded.

Is my script saved automatically?+

Yes. The standalone app, the Plasma widget and the GNOME extension save your script and reading position on your computer as you type. Nothing is uploaded. Only this browser demo resets when you reload it.

Is it ready for an important live presentation?+

This is a development preview. Recognition can lose your place, and real-device testing is incomplete. Keep a copy of your notes, learn the manual controls and rehearse on your own hardware first.
